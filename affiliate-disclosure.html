Last updated: July 30, 2026

BrowseWise participates in affiliate marketing programs, which means we may earn a commission when you click a "View Deal" link and make a qualifying purchase — at no extra cost to you. This is how we keep BrowseWise free to use.

How it works

Each product page links out to a retailer such as Amazon, AliExpress, Temu, or another partner. If you buy something after clicking through, the retailer's affiliate program may pay us a small referral fee. Prices you pay are not affected by this arrangement.

How this affects our recommendations

We aim to feature products based on genuine usefulness and value, not solely on commission rates. That said, we only feature products that are part of affiliate programs we've joined, so our catalog is inherently limited to those retailers and programs.

Our commitment

  • We clearly label every outbound product link as a "View Deal" affiliate link.
  • We never accept payment in exchange for a specific rating or review.
  • We update or remove listings that no longer represent good value.
